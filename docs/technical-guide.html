Technical Guide

How the ATE Social Survey Workflow is built: EarthRanger ingestion, answer cleaning and relabelling, binning, sentiment scoring, statistical comparisons, maps, PNG conversion, and assembly of the Word report and dashboard.

Download the full Technical Guide (PDF)

This guide was written directly from spec.yaml. Three behaviours are worth knowing up front:

  • Grouping is fixed to none (groupers: []), so every output covers the whole dataset.
  • The event fetch uses raise_on_empty: true, so a time range with no survey events fails the run.
  • The sentiment score counts I Don't Know and No Response as 0, and those zeros are included in each respondent's mean.

Sentiment scoring at a glance

AnswerNegative statements (6)Positive statements (3)
Strongly Disagree51
Disagree42
Neutral33
Agree24
Strongly Agree15
I Don't Know / No Response00

The workflow takes the mean of the 9 scores, rounds it to 1 decimal place, groups it into 5 equal-interval bins, and maps the bins 0 - 1 … 4 - 5 to Strongly Disagree … Strongly Agree. Higher scores mean a more positive attitude towards elephants.
